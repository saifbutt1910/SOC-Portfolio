Week 1 Day 9 - Encryption and Common Attacks

What is Encryption?

Encryption converts readable data into unreadable data so that unauthorized people cannot access it.

Why Encryption is Important

- Protects passwords
- Protects personal information
- Protects financial data
- Maintains privacy

Common Cyber Attacks

Phishing
Tricks users into revealing sensitive information.

Malware
Malicious software designed to damage or compromise systems.

Ransomware
Encrypts victim files and demands payment.

DoS/DDoS
Overwhelms a service with traffic to make it unavailable.

Social Engineering
Manipulates people into revealing information or performing unsafe actions.

What I Learned Today

- Encryption protects the contents of data.
- Firewalls control network traffic.
- Cyber attacks can target both systems and people.
