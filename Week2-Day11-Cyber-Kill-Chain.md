Week 2 Day 11 - Cyber Kill Chain

What is the Cyber Kill Chain?

The Cyber Kill Chain is a framework that describes the stages of a cyber attack.

The Seven Stages

1. Reconnaissance
2. Weaponization
3. Delivery
4. Exploitation
5. Installation
6. Command and Control
7. Actions on Objectives

Why It Is Important

The framework helps security teams understand where an attack is occurring and where it can be stopped.

What I Learned Today

- Cyber attacks happen in multiple stages.
- Detecting attacks early reduces damage.
- SOC analysts use the Kill Chain to understand attacker progress.
