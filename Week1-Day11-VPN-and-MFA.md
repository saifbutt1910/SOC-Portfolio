Week 1 Day 11 - VPN and Multi-Factor Authentication

What is a VPN?

A VPN (Virtual Private Network) creates an encrypted connection between a device and the internet.

Benefits of a VPN
- Protects data on public networks
- Improves privacy
- Encrypts internet traffic

What is MFA?

Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) requires more than one form of verification before granting access.

Authentication Factors

1. Something you know
   - Password
   - PIN

2. Something you have
   - Phone
   - Security token

3. Something you are
   - Fingerprint
   - Face recognition

What I Learned Today

- VPNs help protect internet traffic.
- MFA adds an extra layer of security.
- Passwords alone are often not enough to secure accounts.
