Week 1 Day 8 - Firewalls

What is a Firewall?

A firewall is a security system that monitors and controls incoming and outgoing network traffic.

What Does a Firewall Do?

- Allows legitimate traffic
- Blocks suspicious traffic
- Helps protect systems from unauthorized access

Allow vs Block

Allow = Traffic is permitted

Block = Traffic is denied

What I Learned Today

- Firewalls act as a protective barrier between trusted and untrusted networks.
- They help reduce the risk of attacks.
- Firewalls are one of the most important security controls in cybersecurity.
