Week 1 Day 14 - Incident Response

What is Incident Response?

Incident Response is the process of detecting, managing, and recovering from security incidents.

Incident Response Stages

Identification
Determine what happened.

Containment
Stop the incident from spreading.

Eradication
Remove the threat.

Recovery
Restore systems to normal operation.

Why Incident Response is Important

- Reduces damage
- Helps organizations recover quickly
- Improves security posture

What I Learned Today

- Security incidents must be handled systematically.
- Incident Response follows a structured process.
- SOC analysts play an important role in incident response.
