Week 2 Day 10 - MITRE ATT&CK Basics

What is MITRE ATT&CK?

MITRE ATT&CK is a framework that documents common attacker tactics and techniques.

Tactic

A tactic represents the attacker's objective.

Examples:
- Initial Access
- Execution
- Persistence
- Credential Access

Technique

A technique is the method used to achieve a tactic.

Examples:
- Phishing
- PowerShell execution
- Credential dumping

Why It Is Important

SOC analysts use MITRE ATT&CK to understand attacker behavior and improve detection and response.

What I Learned Today

- Tactics describe attacker goals.
- Techniques describe how attackers achieve those goals.
- MITRE ATT&CK helps analysts investigate incidents more effectively.
