Week 1 Day 10 - CIA Triad and Access Control

CIA Triad

Confidentiality
Protecting information from unauthorized access.

Integrity
Ensuring data remains accurate and unchanged.

Availability
Ensuring systems and data are accessible when needed.

Authentication

Authentication verifies a user's identity.

Examples:
- Username and password
- Fingerprint
- Face recognition

Authorization

Authorization determines what actions a user is allowed to perform after authentication.

What I Learned Today

- The CIA Triad is a core cybersecurity model.
- Authentication proves identity.
- Authorization controls permissions.
- These concepts are important in protecting systems and data.
