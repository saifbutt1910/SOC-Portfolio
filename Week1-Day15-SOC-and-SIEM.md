Week 1 Day 15 - SOC and SIEM Basics

What is a SOC?

A Security Operations Center (SOC) is a team responsible for monitoring and responding to cybersecurity threats.

What Does a SOC Analyst Do?

- Monitor alerts
- Investigate logs
- Identify threats
- Escalate incidents
- Document findings

What is a SIEM?

SIEM stands for Security Information and Event Management.

A SIEM collects, stores and analyzes logs from different systems.

Benefits of SIEM

- Centralized log management
- Faster threat detection
- Easier investigations
- Automated alerts

What I Learned Today

- SOC teams monitor and respond to security incidents.
- SIEM tools help analysts manage large amounts of log data.
- Logs and alerts are essential for threat detection.
